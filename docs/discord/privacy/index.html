Phantom World Discord Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Phantom Hosting ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you interact with the Phantom World Discord Bot (the "Bot") and related services at billing.phantom-chicken.com.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Bot, we may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user ID, username, server (guild) ID, channel ID, and roles needed for commands
  • Command data: text you submit to slash commands (e.g. status requests, alerts, music searches)
  • Server events: join/leave notifications and FiveM server status (player counts, server name)
  • Technical logs: timestamps, error logs, and IP addresses on our VPS for security and abuse prevention
  • Billing data: if you purchase hosting, see our main Privacy Policy

2. How we use information

  • Operate Bot commands and community features
  • Display FiveM server status and send invite links
  • Moderation, anti-abuse, and service reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Direct messages

With your permission (or server admin authorization), the Bot may send you direct messages such as server invites or alerts. You can disable DMs from server members in Discord settings; some messages may not be deliverable if blocked.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Discord — as required to operate the Bot (see Discord Privacy Policy)
  • Hosting providers — servers that run the Bot and game infrastructure
  • Law enforcement — when required by law or to protect safety

5. Retention

We retain logs and command-related data only as long as needed for operations, security, and legal compliance, then delete or anonymize where practical.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of data we hold about you where applicable law provides these rights. Contact us via Contact Us.

7. Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. International users

Data may be processed in the United States or where our servers are located.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
