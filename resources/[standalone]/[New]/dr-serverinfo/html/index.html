Phantom World — Help

Welcome

Phantom World is a freeroam-first Los Santos — jobs on demand, phone contracts, dealers across the map, and light RP. Open this board anytime with F1 or /help.

Discord: discord.gg/Z9Mxu72zZ6 · Join: cfx.re/join/3m87mo

First Join

  1. Friendly loadscreen → city tour (BACKSPACE skips) → multichar → outfit → spawn
  2. Starter pack: modest bank/cash + supplies + one free economy ride (/startercar). Dealers use real-world prices.
  3. Press F10 for the Interaction Hub (jobs, cars, houses, gangs, phone…)

Cars & Dealers

  • Starter: /startercar — one free economy/used ride (no sports/armored)
  • PDM Legion · Luxury Autos Rockford · Boats docks · Aircraft LSIA · Trucks port
  • Self-service — no cardealer job required. Call owned cars with F9

Jobs & Contracts

  • Job board NPC near Legion — Press E (trucker, taxi, garbage, tow, mechanic, burgershot, security, recycle…)
  • LSPD / EMS desks for freeroam clock-in
  • Underground contact for phone contracts
  • GTAO-style calls: hitman, robbery, repo, bounty — test with /jobcall

Housing

Homes use next_housing. Look for for-sale property markers. Staff can seed listings and enable direct purchase in /nexthousing.

Banking & Shops

  • Banks / ATMs — press E (omes banking)
  • 24/7, Liquor, Ammu-Nation, Clothing — press E
  • Phone banking via NPWD apps

Rank & Membership

Earn XP as you play (XNL rank bar). Membership tiers / coins: /membership. Weapon rank gates are off for freeroam.
