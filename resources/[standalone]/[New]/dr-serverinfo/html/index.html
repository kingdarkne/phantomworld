Server Info & Help

Welcome

This guide explains jobs, activities, keybinds, the XP system, and how to get around. You can open this anytime with F1 or /help. If you changed keybinds, check Settings > Key Bindings > FiveM.

About This Server

Phantom World is a QBCore roleplay server. Enjoy the city: get a job, buy a house, run businesses, do heists, and earn RP (experience) as you play. Join the Discord for rules, updates, and support: discord.gg/phantomworld.

XP & Rank (RP) System

How it works: You earn RP (experience points) by doing jobs and making money. The more you work and get paid, the more XP you gain. Your rank is shown on-screen (XNLRankBar) and is saved to your character.

  • Earning XP: When you receive money from a job (paychecks, sales, deliveries, etc.), you automatically get a small amount of RP. Different jobs give slightly different XP rates (e.g. police/EMS/mechanic a bit more per dollar).
  • Rank bar: The on-screen rank/XP bar shows your progress. Leveling up reflects your activity on the server.
  • Admins: Staff can give RP with /givexp <amount> (themselves) or /givexp <player id> <amount> (e.g. /givexp 5 5000).

XP is tied to your character and persists across sessions. There is no separate “level” command for players — just play jobs and earn money to gain RP.

Membership & Coins (Supporter Perks)

Membership tiers: none, bronze, silver, gold, diamond. Higher tiers unlock extra perks and access to some custom vehicles.

  • Free trial: Every brand new character gets a free 30-day Gold membership, starter money, RP boost, and basic items to help you get going.
  • After trial: When the trial expires, your tier returns to none. You can keep playing normally or upgrade again via the store.
  • Coins: Special currency used for supporter perks (e.g. custom cars). Your balance is stored per character.
  • Check status: Use /membership in chat to see your tier and coin balance.
  • How to get more: Support the server via the official Tebex store (link in Discord). After purchase, staff will apply your tier/coins in-game.
  • Staff tools: Admins manage perks with /setmembership, /addcoins, and /removecoins.

Membership and coins never give god-mode or rule-breaking powers. They are for supporter perks like cosmetics and access to selected custom vehicles.

Banking (Phantom)

Phantom's Banking — deposit, withdraw, transfer, and view history.

  • At a bank: Go to any Phantom Banking blip on the map, find the banker or use the zone, press E to open the bank.
  • ATMs: Go to any Phantom ATM (blip or in-world ATM), press E. First time: set a 4-digit PIN; then use it to access your account.
  • Phone: Open Phone (M) → tap Welcome Phantom (bank app) for the full bank UI anywhere.

Use /bank to open banking from anywhere (if enabled).

What You Can Do

  • Housing: Use ps-housing — find property blips or use the phone/housing menu to buy or enter homes. Inside a property, press Z to open the furniture store and decorate.
  • Apartments: Spawn at apartments; use qb-apartments to enter your apartment.
  • Burger Shot: Work at or visit Burger Shot (y_burgershot) for food and job.
  • Police / EMS: Get hired at City Hall or the station; use job-specific items and vehicles (MDT, stashes, etc.).
  • Mechanics: LS Customs, Beeker's Garage, Benny's — repair and customize vehicles.
  • Heists / Robberies: Fleeca (dr-fleeca), Ammo (dr-ammorobbery), Store (lation_247robbery) — follow in-game prompts at locations.
  • World Events: Random earthquakes (1-3h intervals) shake the city. Admins can trigger events via F7 World Events Panel.
  • Garages & vehicles: Go to Parking or Garage blips, press E to take out or store vehicles. Depots for impounded vehicles.
  • Clothing & Barber: Visit Clothing and Barber blips to change appearance.
  • Driving school: DR Driving School — get your license.
  • Animations: Use the animation menu (dr-animationmenu) to play emotes.
  • Rental vehicles: dr-rental — rent cars at rental locations.
  • Phone: Messages, contacts, banking (Welcome Phantom), garage, jobs, ads, Twitter, etc.

Who Can Use What

  • All players: Phone (M), inventory (TAB), server info (F1), banking, jobs, garages, housing, /ask <question> for AI help.
  • Admin only: /admin (admin menu), /givexp, /givemoney, /giveitem, /car, /givekeys, /setjob, and other admin commands. vMenu (N) is usually restricted to staff.
  • Owners / Gods: Full server control; all admin commands plus server console tools.

If a command says “no permission” or doesn’t work, you don’t have access. Ask staff or check Discord for roles.

Quick Tips

  • Get a job at City Hall (blip on map) or apply at the business (e.g. Burger Shot, mechanic shop).
  • Earn XP (RP) by working and making money — see the XP & Rank section above.
  • Garages: drive to a Parking blip and press E to open the garage menu.
  • Banking: use Welcome Phantom on your phone, or visit a bank/ATM and press E.
  • No admin around? Use /ask <question> to get help from the AI assistant (e.g. /ask How do I get a job?).
  • Need more help? Open this guide with F1, or ask in server Discord: discord.gg/phantomworld.
